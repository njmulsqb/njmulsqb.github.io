# About

I am a security engineer and builder focused on application security, cloud security, and practical engineering work that reduces risk without unnecessary complexity.

I like simple systems, careful thinking, and tools that help people do difficult work with less friction.

My work sits between engineering and security: helping teams understand risk, build with intent, and ship software they can trust.

I work with privacy, open-source tooling, and security practices that are usable in the real world—especially for teams that want to move quickly without creating fragile systems.
